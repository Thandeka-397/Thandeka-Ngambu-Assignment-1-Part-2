Brief History

Edu-Motherwell Development Organisation was founded in 2008 in Gqeberha. This organisation assists in community-level of having crises of youth unemployment and low-educational resources in the communities.

Mission Statement

It assists youth to find and develop their talents, and to realize their potential in life. It creates opportunities for impoverished particularly youth to get a quality education.

Vision Statement

Is to overflow South African youth communities, local graduates to break the cycle of poverty. Serving as role models for future generations and drive community transformation.

Edu-Motherwell

Providing educational support, community upliftment, and development resources to empower vulnerable groups in Motherwell, Gqeberha.
